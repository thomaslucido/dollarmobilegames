## Top trends

Only two non-puzzle trends have any evidence. The other slots were left empty rather than invented.

1. **Arcade quick-score action** (non-puzzle; confidence: low-medium)
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 (via PocketGamer.biz) says arcade grew in the US in H1 2026, one of four US genres to grow (puzzle, party, kids and arcade). AppMagic H1 2025 had US arcade up 52% year on year, the top Y/Y growth rate in the US that year, at about $287m. Smash Fest! appears near the top of an Appvulture free-games list (Nov 2026). Its genre is not confirmed, so treat it as a name to check, not as proof of this trend.
   - Chart movement: no rank history available. Smash Fest! is listed among the free-chart leaders on Appvulture only.
   - Core verb: tap or hold to dodge, fire or time a hit. Score-chasing.
   - Session length: 1-3 min. Why players like it: instant restart, a clear best score, and short bursts that suit a $0.99 app.
   - Risk: arcade is one of the few genres with a conflicting signal. A 2024 opt-in data set has arcade falling from 50% to 46%. The H1 2026 and 2025 AppMagic figures are the better sources.

2. **Kids-friendly casual play** (non-puzzle; confidence: low)
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 says kids games grew in the US. No percentage was given. Statista's June 2025 US kids downloads ranking is older context only.
   - Chart movement: none found for titles in this genre.
   - Core verb: tap to pick, match or build for a simple reward.
   - Session length: 2-5 min.
   - Why players like it: low friction and an easy-to-read goal.
   - Constraint: must stay 4+, with no data collection, ads, accounts or purchases, which limits the usual kids-game model. Treat this as a watch item.

## Watch, do not build yet
- **Party games:** AppMagic says party grew about 29% globally in H1 2026, but the US is a weak market for it (13% of its contribution). Not a US trend.
- **Strategy-lite lane defense:** Tintrot (2026-11-09) already covers it. Strategy download growth (+14.5%) is from 2024-era data and conflicts with PocketGamer.biz H1 2026 "strategy stumbles".
- **Rhythm, physics and word games:** no 2026 growth data found in three searches. Not reported as trends.
