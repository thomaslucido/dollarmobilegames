## Top trends (not in the last 6 genres)

1. **Word games (Scrabble-style and daily word) (confidence: low)**
   - Evidence: NYT Games' standalone multiplayer word game Crossplay is reported at over 1M downloads (PocketGamer.biz listing). A Sensor Tower Q4 2025 page shows Zen Word rising in US Android downloads late in the quarter. Word is not named as a growth genre in the H1 2026 AppMagic or PocketGamer.biz summaries.
   - Chart movement: none verified for US iPhone.
   - Core verb: drag or tap letters to form words. Session length: 3-8 min.
   - Why players like it: a clear goal, a daily-return habit, and low skill ceiling.
   - Risk: word is usually grouped under puzzle, which holds about 44% of casual revenue. Confirm whether it is a separate lane before the Designer treats it as new. Avoid any Scrabble or NYT-style mechanic and naming.

2. **Kids-friendly casual play (confidence: low)**
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 says kids games grew in the US. No percentage was given. Carried over from 2026-11-14 with no new data.
   - Core verb: tap to pick, match, or build for a simple reward. Session length: 2-5 min.
   - Constraint: must stay 4+, with no data collection, ads, accounts, or purchases. This limits the usual kids-game model.

3. **Party and multiplayer-lite (confidence: low, weak US signal)**
   - Evidence: AppMagic says party grew about 29% globally in H1 2026. PocketGamer.biz H1 2026 summary lists party as one of four US genres to grow. The US share of party contribution is small (about 13%).
   - Core verb: quick turn-based or hot-seat taps for a group. Session length: 3-5 min.
   - Constraint: our games run fully offline with no accounts, so multiplayer is out of scope. Only a local pass-and-play variant would fit. Treat as a watch item, not a build candidate.

Only three slots were filled, and none of them has strong evidence. Better to flag weak signals than to invent rankings.

- **Rhythm:** no standalone growth data found. The only hit was an event mode inside Marvel Contest of Champions. Not a trend.
