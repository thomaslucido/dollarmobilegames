# Scout report 2026-10-26

## Sourcing caveat
- `data/trends/` holds only `.gitkeep` (0 chart snapshots), so this report uses web search (2 of 3 searches used). No October 2026 chart data was found. The newest figures are H1 2026 and 2025 full-year industry summaries, which are directional. Several sources conflict; percentages are approximate.
- Re-run once `charts.yml` has produced 2+ snapshots in `data/trends/`.

## Top 5 rising trends

1. **Sort puzzle (color sorting, tubes, stacks)**
   - Evidence: Sort Puzzle passed Blast as the third-largest puzzle subgenre by IAP revenue at the start of 2026 (Mobidictum). Cited as a rising hybrid-casual mechanic.
   - Core verb: tap a source container, then tap a target to pour or move the top piece.
   - Session: 3-8 min. Why: readable at a glance, satisfying reveals, endless levels.

2. **Block puzzle (grid fill and line clear)**
   - Evidence: PocketGamer.biz reports a sharp rise in Block Puzzle downloads from January to summer 2026, and says the subgenre now looks established. Revenue is weaker than downloads because much of it is ad-driven. Block Blast! was #1 in a January 2026 download ranking.
   - Core verb: drag a piece onto a grid and clear full lines.
   - Session: 3-10 min. Why: familiar, calm, endless.

3. **Screw / cut-rope physics puzzle**
   - Evidence: Cited as one of the fastest-growing hybrid-casual genres in 2026 (Mobidictum, Cinevva). Smash Fest! jumped from #6 to #1 on the FoxData July 2026 download chart. Chart-level evidence is thin.
   - Core verb: drag a line or tap to cut, release, or topple, and watch physics finish the job.
   - Session: 1-3 min. Why: instant physical feedback and spectacle.

4. **Hybrid-casual puzzle with a light upgrade layer**
   - Evidence: Hybrid-casual in-app revenue rose about 20% to $4.2B in 2025, while the overall casual market grew only about 1.3% and downloads fell about 7%. Hybrid-casual puzzles are the standout. Newer puzzle titles (Color Block Jam, Pixel Flow) are reaching top-grossing charts while Match-3 is called saturated.
   - Core verb: solve a short puzzle, then spend coins on a persistent upgrade that makes the next round easier.
   - Session: 5-10 min. Why: short rounds plus a reason to return.

5. **Idle / cozy idle collection**
   - Evidence: Devsisters' CookieRun: Crumble (idle-inspired, due later in 2026). Cats & Soup reported past 60M downloads (Growth Market Reports summary, carried over from 2026-10-24). No new H2 2026 idle data found this week.
   - Core verb: tap to collect or upgrade, then leave it running.
   - Session: 1-3 min active, with offline progress. Why: low pressure, rewards while away.
   - Confidence: lower than the four above.

Context: PocketGamer.biz H1 2026 puts puzzle revenue up about 20% YoY to $7.6bn (now #2 genre). Strategy still leads revenue (about $9.1bn in H1 2026, AppMagic) but is too heavy for a $0.99 app. Overall IAP is up about 10% while downloads are flat, so growth comes from engagement, not new installs.

Genre spacing: the last 4 registry genres are slingshot golf, one-touch flyer, hex arrow puzzle, and merge puzzle. None of the five trends above repeats them. Trend 1 (sort) and trend 2 (block) are both grid or tube puzzles, so pick only one of them per build night.
