## Top trends

1. **Cozy stress-relief tending sim** (confidence: low-medium)
   - Evidence: Plarium says US and European players increasingly cite "stress relief" as their main motivation. AppFollow reports "finally a game I can play without stress" reviews across many unrelated games. Gossip Harbor (cozy story/merge hybrid, top 5 grossing worldwide in Oct 2026) is up 95% YoY Jan-Oct, but it is merge-led, so it is evidence of demand for cozy, not a template.
   - Core verb: tap to tend, harvest or decorate a small world.
   - Session: 5-10 min. Why: low pressure, charm, no fail-state stress (we still need a goal and a best score per PLAYBOOK 4.2).

2. **Light lane-placement strategy (tower-defence lite)** (confidence: low)
   - Evidence: a 2026 marketing report says strategy downloads rose 14.5%. Last War and Whiteout Survival sit in the top grossing lists, but they are large, IAP-heavy titles and a poor model for a $0.99 app.
   - Core verb: place a unit or tower on a lane, then time one upgrade.
   - Session: 3-5 min. Why: clear decisions and a clear fail state.

3. **Hybrid-casual route-or-place puzzle with meta progression** (confidence: low-medium)
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 says puzzle is 44% of casual player spend ($4.9B H1) and hybrid-casual was the only casual segment to grow in-app revenue. Royal Match (top 5 grossing worldwide, Oct 2026) shows the meta-progression puzzle model at scale. Match-3 itself is described as plateauing, so do not build a match-3 clone.
   - Core verb: tap or drag to route or place pieces, then spend stars or coins on unlocks between levels.
   - Session: 3-8 min. Why: familiar puzzle loop with progression reasons to return.
   - Note: must not overlap hex arrow (Burrowbank), sort (Griddlewise), block (Glasswright) or merge (Wheelthrown).

Two further slots were left unfilled because the searches produced no evidence for them. We should not invent trends to fill them.

## Trends to skip
- **One-touch flyer**: covered by Lantern Drift.
- **Slingshot mini golf**: covered by Bank Shot.
- **Hex arrow puzzle**: covered by Burrowbank.
- **Merge puzzle**: covered by Wheelthrown. Gossip Harbor-style merge/story titles also fall here.
- **Sort puzzle**: covered by Griddlewise.
- **Block puzzle roguelike**: covered by Glasswright.
- **Idle runner**: covered by Sow Far (2026-11-02).
- **Casino / gambling-style titles**: excluded. Gambling mechanics break the 4+ rating rule.
- **Match-3**: maturing per AppMagic; not a fresh signal.
- **Genre repeat check**: the last 6 games include merge, sort, hex arrow, one-touch flyer, slingshot golf and idle runner. Trends 1 and 2 are not in that list. Trend 3 is a puzzle and must pass the 6-game rule when the Designer picks.

## Strongest candidates for the Designer
- Cozy stress-relief tending sim (best fit with Sow Far's riso look only if the palette and verb differ clearly).
- Hybrid-casual route-or-place puzzle with meta progression (largest revenue signal, but puzzle-saturated).
