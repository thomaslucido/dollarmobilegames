Trend evidence otherwise comes from H1 2026 industry reports (AppMagic, Sensor Tower via trade blogs). Several figures conflict between sources; percentages below are approximate.

## Top 5 rising trends

1. **Hybrid-casual puzzle (puzzle + light upgrades)**
   - Evidence: Hybrid-casual is the standout growth segment in H1 2026 (Sensor Tower data via trade press: about $2.4B IAP, up 23%, while total mobile game IAP fell 2%). AppMagic describes hybrid-casual puzzles as a rising story, citing Color Block Jam and Screw Jam.
   - Core verb: varies, but a persistent upgrade or progression layer sits on top of a simple puzzle action.
   - Session: 5-10 min. Why: short rounds plus a reason to come back to upgrade.

2. **Merge + progression (kingdom and story merge)**
   - Evidence: Similarweb US top grossing (Oct 19, 2026) has Gossip Harbor: Merge & Story in the top 5. Merge is cited as the fastest-growing casual puzzle subgenre in AppMagic's H1 2026 report (+74% YoY, though the revenue figures conflict between two summaries).
   - Core verb: drag one item onto a matching one to combine it.
   - Session: 2-5 min. Why: low skill floor, constant small rewards.

3. **Idle and cozy idle sims moving into mainstream casual**
   - Evidence: Devsisters' CookieRun: Crumble (idle-inspired, due later in 2026). Cats & Soup, a cozy idle sim, reported past 60M downloads (Growth Market Reports summary).
   - Core verb: tap to collect or upgrade, then leave it running.
   - Session: 1-3 min active, with offline progress. Why: low pressure, rewards while away.

4. **Block puzzle (classic grid fill)**
   - Evidence: Block Blast! held the download lead on the FoxData July 2026 global chart before Smash Fest! took #1. Azur Games reported a sharp rise in its Block Puzzle line from January to summer 2026.
   - Core verb: drag a piece onto a grid and clear full lines.
   - Session: 3-10 min. Why: familiar, calm, endless.

5. **Physics destruction and stacking**
   - Evidence: FoxData July 2026 downloads: Smash Fest! jumped from #6 to #1 (6.41M downloads). Weakly sourced at chart level; no dedicated physics-genre report was found this week.
   - Core verb: aim and launch, or tap to break and topple structures.
   - Session: 1-3 min. Why: spectacle and instant physical feedback.

Context: PocketGamer.biz H1 2026 puts puzzle revenue up about 20% YoY to $7.6bn (now #2 genre). Casual in-app revenue grew only about 1.3% in 2025 while downloads fell about 7%, so growth is coming from deeper engagement rather than new installs. Strategy hybrids (kingdom or base builders with mini-games, e.g. Kingshot) are rising but are heavy for a $0.99 app.

## Notes for Designer
- Originality: use trends only. Do not copy any named title's theme, art, or mechanics. Evidence titles are for trend context only, and `scripts/validate.mjs` checks names against `data/trends/` once snapshots exist.
- Genre spacing: the last 6 games rule has little history so far (3 games in the registry, 2 of them flyer and arrow-type). Hybrid-casual puzzle and idle are the cleanest non-overlapping options.
- Recommended lean: hybrid-casual puzzle or merge + progression have the strongest multi-source support this week. Physics and block puzzle have weaker or older evidence.
