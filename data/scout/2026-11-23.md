# Scout report 2026-11-23

## Sourcing caveat
- `data/trends/` holds only `.gitkeep`. No chart snapshots exist, so this report cannot rank by chart movement.
- Web search used 2 of 3 allowed queries. Results were a November 2026 top-grossing search (no US iPhone November 2026 chart published yet; latest is October 2026 worldwide) and a genre-trend search (H1 2026 data).
- All trends below are directional. Re-run once `charts.yml` has produced 2+ snapshots.

## Latest market signals
- **Worldwide top grossing, October 2026 (Sensor Tower):** Roblox, Honor of Kings, Gossip Harbor, Royal Match, Whiteout Survival.
- **AppMagic, October 2026:** Gossip Harbor cooling after an August peak; Monopoly Go IAP sliding for a third straight month.
- **Most downloaded, October 2026:** Free Fire, then Roblox.
- **Genre, H1 2026 (AppMagic / PocketGamer.biz):** puzzle earned about $7.6B (+20% YoY, about 44% of casual spend). Match-3 is about 51% of puzzle spend. Merge reached about $1.3B. Hybrid-casual is described as a growth engine.
- **Idle:** mixed. Revenue can grow while installs flatten (MapleStory: Idle RPG cited, but that is a midcore title).

## Top trends (directional, low to medium confidence)

1. **Match-3 / tile-matching puzzle (confidence: medium on direction, low on an open slot)**
   - Evidence: Royal Match and Gossip Harbor in the Oct 2026 global top five; match-3 about half of puzzle spend (AppMagic H1 2026).
   - Core verb: swap or match tiles.
   - Session length: 5-10 min. Why players like it: large, reliable revenue lane with strong progression.
   - Caveat: this is the most saturated lane. Market signal only, not a recommended slot.

2. **Idle tap-to-upgrade builder (confidence: low-medium)**
   - Evidence: idle revenue growth while installs plateau (AppMagic / analyst commentary, H1 2026); MapleStory: Idle RPG as the standout example.
   - Core verb: tap to collect and spend on upgrades.
   - Session length: 2-5 min, with return visits for offline gains.
   - Distinct from Sow Far (idle garden lane runner). Must avoid RPG combat and any purchase-style currency.

3. **Kids-friendly casual play (confidence: low)**
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 says kids games grew in the US, with no percentage given. Carried from 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-16 with no new data.
   - Core verb: tap to pick, match or build for a simple reward.
   - Constraint: 4+, no data collection, no ads, no accounts, no purchases. This rules out the usual kids-game model.

4. **Party / pass-and-play (confidence: low, watch only)**
   - Evidence: party grew in the US in H1 2026 (PocketGamer.biz), but the US share is small (about 13%).
   - Only a local hot-seat variant fits our offline, no-accounts rules.

5. **Hybrid-casual route-or-place puzzle with meta progression (confidence: low-medium)**
   - Evidence: AppMagic and Sensor Tower on hybrid-casual growth; Adjust reports hyper-casual sessions up 17% in H1 2026.
   - Already the 2026-11-21 pick and marked built in `data/ideas.json`. Listed here for completeness only. Do not repeat it.

## Watch, do not build yet
- Strategy: sources conflict (fastest growing in 2025 vs stumbling in H1 2026). Lane defense (Tintrot) already covers the light end.
- Action RPG, battle royale, shooters: midcore or live-service, not feasible for a $0.99 offline app.
- Rhythm, physics stacking: no 2026 growth data found.

## Trends to skip (already covered or ruled out)
- Covered in `www/games.json` / `data/ideas.json`: one-touch flyer (Lantern Drift), hex arrow (Burrowbank), merge (Wheelthrown), sort (Griddlewise), block roguelike (Glasswright), idle garden runner (Sow Far), cozy tending sim (Simmerstone), lane defense (Tintrot), arcade quick-score (Gulpwort), word glide (Glideleaf), slingshot golf (Bank Shot), hybrid-casual route puzzle (2026-11-21).
- Puzzle-heavy on free charts: Block Blast, Arrows, Magic Sort, match-3. Crowded, so only a clearly distinct verb would be worth considering.
- Casino or gambling-style titles: excluded (4+ rating rule).

## Genre repeat check (no genre repeat within last 6 games)
- Last 6 built: Glasswright (block), Sow Far (idle), Simmerstone (cozy), Tintrot (lane), Gulpwort (arcade), Glideleaf (word).
- Idle tap builder (trend 2) is close to Sow Far's idle family. Designer must give it a clearly different core verb.
- Match-3 (trend 1) is near puzzle titles already built (Burrowbank, Griddlewise, Glasswright, Wheelthrown). Only with a distinct verb.

## Strongest candidates for the Designer
- Idle tap-to-upgrade builder (trend 2): best fit with distinct verb and no shared genre in the last 6 games, but evidence is indirect.
- Kids-friendly casual (trend 3): only if a stronger source appears.
- Do not pick party, shooters or battle royale.
