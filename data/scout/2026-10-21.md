## Top 5 rising trends

1. **Merge puzzles (merge + progression)**
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 casual report says merge is the fastest-growing puzzle subgenre (+74% YoY, reported as $127M net; a second AppMagic summary gives $1.3B for the same genre, so the figures conflict). August 2026 top-grossing roundup lists casual merge titles among trending apps. Gossip Harbor cited as a top performer.
   - Core verb: drag/drop one item onto a matching one.
   - Session: 2-5 min. Why: low skill floor, constant small rewards.

2. **Physics destruction / stacking**
   - Evidence: FoxData July 2026 global downloads chart: Smash Fest! jumped #6 to #1 (6.41M downloads), displacing Block Blast!.
   - Core verb: aim/launch or tap to break and topple structures.
   - Session: 1-3 min. Why: spectacle, instant feedback, satisfying physics.

3. **Arrow / one-direction puzzles**
   - Evidence: FoxData July 2026 says the "Arrow Puzzle" trend stays strong with several similar titles climbing.
   - Core verb: tap or swipe to clear a path for pieces.
   - Session: 1-3 min per level. Why: readable rules, easy to learn in one tap.

4. **Block puzzle (classic grid fill)**
   - Evidence: Azur Games report flags a sharp rise in its Block Puzzle line Jan to summer 2026. Block Blast! held the download lead before July.
   - Core verb: drag a piece onto a grid, clear lines.
   - Session: 3-10 min. Why: familiar, calm, endless.

5. **Hybrid-casual puzzle (puzzle + light upgrades)**
   - Evidence: a trade blog (projectwet, undated) says hyper-casual is moving toward hybrid-casual with upgrades and progression. Weak source; the 2023 Sensor Tower note is dated.
   - Core verb: varies; adds a persistent upgrade layer.
   - Session: 5-10 min. Why: progression keeps players returning.

Context: PocketGamer.biz H1 2026 analysis: puzzle revenue up ~20% YoY to $7.6bn, now #2 genre. Installs for top titles fell YoY while revenue rose. Match-3 is about half of casual puzzle spend (AppMagic). Idle has no 2026 evidence found.
