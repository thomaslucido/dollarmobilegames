# Scout report 2026-11-02

## Sourcing caveat
- `data/trends/` has no chart snapshots, so this report uses web search (3 of 3 searches used). Searches did not return a verified November 2026 US iPhone Games chart. The strongest dated US chart signal is Apple's 2025 year-end list (BlockBlast! top free game). Marlvel.ai (third-party) shows MONOPOLY GO! and Last War at #1 top grossing in spring 2026, but no October/November data was found. Treat everything below as directional.
- Re-run once `charts.yml` has produced 2+ snapshots in `data/trends/`.

## Top 5 rising trends

1. **Hybrid-casual idle / meta-progression runner**
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 (via Casual Games Report and Game Dev Reports) says hybrid-casual was the only casual segment to grow in-app revenue (up 20% to $4.2B). Hypercasual is fading per Appodeal's 2025 benchmark. Growth is concentrated in hybrid-casual puzzle and idle-style meta loops.
   - Core verb: hold or tap to run or build, then spend earnings on upgrades between runs.
   - Session: 3-6 min. Why: low skill entry, visible progress, a reason to come back. Confidence: medium.

2. **Cozy tending / small-world sim**
   - Evidence: Mobi.gg names Winter Burrow the best cosy game of October 2026, and cosy titles recur in the same month's roundups. No chart data.
   - Core verb: tap to tend, collect and decorate a small world.
   - Session: 5-10 min. Why: low pressure, charm, visual variety. Confidence: low (editorial pick only).

3. **Roguelike deck-builder (card and tactics)**
   - Evidence: Mobi.gg names Dice A Million a standout October 2026 release. Card games show on grossing lists but are not a clear breakout.
   - Core verb: pick one card or tile per turn to shape a run.
   - Session: 4-8 min per run. Why: clear choices, short runs, replayable. Confidence: low-medium.
   - Note: roguelike is already covered by Glasswright. Keep only as a sub-idea if the Designer wants a non-roguelike angle.

4. **Casual strategy (light tower-defence / lane placement)**
   - Evidence: a 2026 marketing report says strategy downloads rose 14.5%. The standouts it names (Kingshot, Last War, MONOPOLY GO!) are large titles, a poor model for a $0.99 app with a 3-minute loop.
   - Core verb: place units or towers on a lane and time upgrades.
   - Session: 3-5 min. Why: deep decisions, clear fail state. Confidence: low.

5. **Hybrid-casual puzzle with a meta layer (non-block, non-merge, non-sort)**
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 says hybrid-casual growth is concentrated in puzzle. Royal Kingdom ($510M+ lifetime IAP) and Royal Match ($465M) show the meta-progression puzzle model at scale. Those are large titles, so this is a mechanic signal only.
   - Core verb: tap or drag to place or route pieces, then unlock and upgrade between levels.
   - Session: 3-8 min. Why: familiar puzzle loop with progression reasons to return. Confidence: low-medium.

## Trends to skip
- **Block puzzle (grid fill / line clear)**: covered by Glasswright. BlockBlast! remains the top US free game in Apple's 2025 list, so expect heavy competition.
- **Roguelike deck-builder**: covered by Glasswright (block puzzle + roguelike).
- **Merge puzzle**: covered by Wheelthrown.
- **Sort puzzle**: covered by Griddlewise.
- **Hex / arrow one-direction puzzles**: covered by Burrowbank.
- **One-touch flyer**: covered by Lantern Drift.
- **Slingshot mini golf**: covered by Bank Shot.
- **Casino and gambling-style titles**: surging per the marketing report, but excluded. Gambling mechanics break the 4+ rating rule in PLAYBOOK.md.
- **Recent-genre repeat check**: the last 6 games include merge, sort, hex arrow, one-touch flyer and slingshot golf. Idle runners, cosy sims and light tower-defence are not in that list. Puzzle with meta progression should be checked against the 6-game rule when the Designer picks.

- Cosy tending sim (fresh signal, not covered, weakest data).
