# Scout report 2026-11-21

- `data/trends/` holds only `.gitkeep`. No chart snapshots exist, so the brief's 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-16 snapshots are not available. This report uses the prior scout reports (2026-11-09, 2026-11-14, 2026-11-16) plus 2 web searches (1 of 3 unused).

- Re-run once `charts.yml` has produced 2+ snapshots.

## Top trends (not in the recent-genre exclusion list)

Recent exclusions: sort, block roguelike, idle runner, cozy tending sim, lane defense, arcade quick-score, word games, plus earlier one-touch flyer, arrow puzzle and merge.

1. **Hybrid-casual route-or-place puzzle with meta progression (confidence: low-medium)**
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 says hybrid-casual is the main casual-adjacent growth story. Sensor Tower calls hybrid casual the new growth engine. Adjust reports hyper-casual sessions up 17% in H1 2026. Royal Match and Gossip Harbor appear in Oct 2026 top-grossing lists (carried from 2026-11-09).
   - Chart movement: none verified.
   - Core verb: tap or drag to route or place a piece, then spend stars or coins on unlocks between levels.
   - Session length: 3-8 min. Why players like it: a familiar puzzle loop with a reason to return between sessions.
   - Risk: puzzle holds about 44% of casual revenue and is crowded. Must not reuse the arrow, sort, block or merge verbs. Must not use coins or stars that suggest purchases.

2. **Puzzle lane, match-3 subgenre (confidence: medium on direction, low on a new slot)**
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 says puzzle earned $4.9B and match-3 is about half of puzzle. PocketGamer.biz H1 2026 puts puzzle at 44% of casual earnings.
   - Core verb: swap or match tiles. This is the largest revenue lane, but it is saturated and our tile-matching would sit close to the match-3 template. Market signal only, not a recommended slot.

3. **Kids-friendly casual play (confidence: low)**
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 says kids games grew in the US, with no percentage given. Carried over from 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-16 with no new data.
   - Core verb: tap to pick, match or build for a simple reward. Session length: 2-5 min.
   - Constraint: must stay 4+, with no data collection, ads, accounts or purchases, which rules out the usual kids-game model. Watch item.

## Watch, do not build yet
- **Party / pass-and-play:** party grew in the US in H1 2026 per PocketGamer.biz, but the US share is small (about 13%). Our games are offline with no accounts, so only a local hot-seat variant would fit.
- **Strategy:** AppMagic and PocketGamer.biz conflict ("fastest growing" in 2025 vs "strategy stumbles" in H1 2026). Lane defense (Tintrot) already covers the light end.
- **Action RPG and battle royale:** new free launches (UnGodly, Monster Hunter Outlanders, PUBG Mobile Flash) show activity, but these are midcore, shooter or live-service games. Not feasible for a $0.99 offline app.
- **Rhythm, physics stacking:** no 2026 growth data found. Not reported as trends.
