# Scout report 2026-10-31

## Sourcing caveat
- `data/trends/` holds only `.gitkeep` (0 chart snapshots), so this report uses web search (2 of 3 searches used). No October 2026 chart data was verified against Apple or Sensor Tower. Figures are H1 2026 industry summaries and third-party chart sites, and several conflict. Treat percentages as directional.
- Re-run once `charts.yml` has produced 2+ snapshots in `data/trends/`.

## Top 5 rising trends

1. **Block puzzle (grid fill and line clear)**
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 says block puzzles were the year's standout hotspot, with over 2,000 games released in H1 (up 89% YoY). Some observers say momentum is cooling. Earlier PocketGamer.biz coverage (cited in the 10-26 report) shows a download rise, with revenue weaker because much of it is ad-driven.
   - Core verb: drag a piece onto a grid and clear full lines.
   - Session: 3-10 min. Why: familiar, calm, endless. Confidence: medium (crowded subgenre).

2. **Hybrid-casual idle runner / meta economy**
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 says hybrid-casual was the only segment to grow in-app revenue (up 20% to $4.2B). Analysts describe idle mechanics appearing inside hybrid titles, such as idle simulations and runners with meta economies. Idle is not tracked as a standalone genre.
   - Core verb: hold or tap to run or build, then spend earnings on upgrades between runs.
   - Session: 3-6 min. Why: low skill entry, visible progress, reason to return. Confidence: medium-low (no clean standalone data).

3. **Roguelike deck-builder (card and tactics)**
   - Evidence: Mobi.gg names Dice A Million, a roguelike deck-builder, as a standout new release of October 2026. Card games appear on the grossing lists (M-S-Y), though they are not a clear breakout.
   - Core verb: pick one card or tile per turn to shape a run.
   - Session: 4-8 min per run. Why: clear choices, short runs, replayable. Confidence: low-medium (one launch, not a chart trend).

4. **Cosy tending / burrow-life sim**
   - Evidence: Mobi.gg names Winter Burrow the best cosy game of October 2026. Cosy and wholesome titles are a recurring theme in the same month's roundups.
   - Core verb: tap to tend, collect, and decorate a small world.
   - Session: 5-10 min. Why: low pressure, a lot of charm, and visual variety. Confidence: low (one editorial pick, no chart data).

5. **Casual strategy (light SLG / tower-style defence)**
   - Evidence: a 2026 marketing report says strategy game downloads rose 14.5%, and that Kingshot became a casual SLG standout. Both are large, heavy titles, which is a poor fit for a $0.99 app with a 3-minute loop.
   - Core verb: place units or towers on a lane and time their upgrades.
   - Session: 3-5 min. Why: deep decisions, clear fail state. Confidence: low (evidence is for large SLG games, not light ones).

## Trends to skip
- **Merge puzzle**: covered by Wheelthrown (built, queued). Merge is also the fastest-growing casual genre, so expect competition.
- **Sort puzzle**: covered by Griddlewise (built). Sort revenue is up 229% YoY, but this is a recent repeat.
- **Hex / arrow one-direction puzzles**: covered by Burrowbank (built).
- **Slingshot mini golf / one-touch flyer**: covered by Bank Shot and Lantern Drift.
- **Casino and gambling-style titles**: surging per the marketing report, but excluded. Gambling mechanics break the 4+ rating rule in PLAYBOOK.md.
- **Recent-genre repeat check**: the last 6 games are slingshot golf, one-touch flyer, hex arrow puzzle, merge puzzle, and pancake sort puzzle. Any card or block-puzzle concept is clear of that list. Idle and cosy are not in it either.

## Strongest candidates for the Designer
- Block puzzle with a twist (most evidence, highest competition).
- Roguelike deck-builder (freshest signal, least evidence).
