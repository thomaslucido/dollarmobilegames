## Top trends

1. **Hybrid-casual route-or-place puzzle with meta progression** (confidence: low-medium)
   - Evidence: AppMagic H1 2026 reports hybrid-casual in-app revenue up about 20% to $4.2B. Royal Match and Gossip Harbor sit in the Oct 2026 top grossing lists, which supports the meta-progression puzzle model. Carried over from the 2026-11-07 scout; no new chart confirmation.
   - Core verb: tap or drag to route or place pieces, then spend stars or coins on unlocks between levels.
   - Session: 3-8 min. Why: familiar puzzle loop with a reason to return between sessions.
   - Note: this is puzzle-heavy. It must stay clear of hex arrow (Burrowbank), sort (Griddlewise), block (Glasswright) and merge (Wheelthrown).

2. **Puzzle revenue growth, merge and block as the leading casual segment** (confidence: medium on direction, low on a new game slot)
   - Evidence: AppMagic Casual Games Report H1 2026 says puzzle and merge keep leading casual growth. PocketGamer.biz H1 2026 says puzzle revenue ramps up while strategy stumbles and RPGs fall.
   - Core verb: varies by sub-genre. Already covered by four of our titles, so this is a market signal, not a new slot.
   - Why it matters: it supports the puzzle trend above, but it adds no open lane of its own.

3. **Light lane-placement strategy (tower-defence lite)** (confidence: low)
   - Evidence: a 2026 marketing report says strategy downloads rose 14.5%. PocketGamer.biz H1 2026 says strategy stumbles, which cuts against this. Last War and Whiteout Survival are large, IAP-heavy titles and a poor model for a $0.99 app.
   - Core verb: place a unit on a lane, then time one upgrade.
   - Session: 3-5 min.

Two slots were left unfilled. Searches found no evidence for them, so no trends were invented.

## Market reads (not trends)
- **US top grossing:** Profitable.app (updated Nov 6 2026) lists MONOPOLY GO!, Clash Royale, eFootball and Disney Solitaire near the top. These are long-running titles, not rising ones. Marlvel and App Radar show MONOPOLY GO! and Last War at the top in spring and August 2026. No new rising title was identified.
- **Installs down, revenue up:** PocketGamer.biz H1 2026 reports installs down year on year while revenue is up in most genres. This favours a monetised $0.99 app only if the loop is strong.
- **Unclear signal:** AppMagic's Nov 3 2026 report says new games in one genre have the highest median lifetime revenue ($4.3m). The source does not make clear which genre it means, so do not use it as a trend.
