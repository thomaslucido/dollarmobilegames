Support

This page covers every game by Tom Lucido on the App Store. Each game is a one-time purchase with no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Moved to a new iPhone? Open the App Store, tap your account, then Purchased, and download the game again for free.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Anything else: email thomaslucido@gmail.com.
