# Lantern Drift (seed game)

- **Trend:** one-touch flyers (hold/release altitude control), a long-running evergreen genre.
- **Twist:** the lantern's flame is a fuel meter. Holding burns it faster; fireflies refill it.
  You can run out of light, not just crash, so every hold is a small decision.
- **Controls:** hold anywhere to rise, release to fall.
- **Scoring:** +1 per willow gap passed, +3 per firefly. Best score saved on device.
- **Difficulty:** scroll speed 150 to 320 px/s over distance; gap 240 down to 150 px; flame drain
  rises slowly with distance.
- **Art:** night lake, palette #0d1230 #2a2457 #5a3a62 sky, #f2a65a lantern, #f8f39a fireflies,
  #3f6b5a willow strands. Everything drawn in canvas.
- **Tagline:** Hold to rise. Let go to fall. Catch every firefly.
- **Store line:** Guide a paper lantern across a night lake and keep its flame alive.
- **Review note:** Free game. Hold the screen to rise, release to fall.
